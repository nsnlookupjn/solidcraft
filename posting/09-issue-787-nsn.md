### Research: inserting military and aerospace parts by NATO Stock Number

**In one line:** let a user type an NSN and get the part in their assembly: standard hardware generated from its spec, other parts from their published dimensions, and a clear placeholder for the rest that the community can fill.

**What it covers**
- **Three tiers:** AN/MS/NAS hardware generated from its spec; other items from their FLIS dimensions; everything else as a labelled envelope placeholder.
- **Free, offline data:** DLA's PUB LOG, with optional online providers compared in the file.
- **Community models:** contributed, reviewed models and properties fill placeholders over time.
- **Builds on what exists:** `parts.insert`, `docs/book/src/dev/libraries.md` and #2071.
- **Export control:** online lookups, supplier links and contributions block sanctioned regions and screen contributors; DLA data has its own terms, and supplier lookups for government supply also need SAM.gov exclusion checks. The only NSN in the file is a public bolt example.

**Disclosure:** I own and operate NSN Lookup Inc (nsnlookup.com), one of the providers compared in the file. It could offer its data free to SolidCraft users, but **the proposal doesn't depend on it.**
