### Research: threads, gears, splines, keyways and broaching

**In one line:** the features shops break the most tools on, or buy in because CAM can't do them, planned from hole or blank to gauge with the physics checked at every step.

**What it covers**
- **Threading as a whole chain:** drill, chamfer, tap or thread mill, cleaning and gauging, planned together instead of placing the thread alone.
- **Threading physics:** tap torque, drill depth against the tap's chamfer, chip room in the flutes, thread-mill deflection and servo lag, and tapered NPT/BSPT threads.
- **In-machine stations:** a tap brush, a cup of tapping compound, and a wear-compensation loop through SolidCraft Edge that keeps thread offsets as they move between jobs and machines.
- **Gear features in CAD:** Gear, Gear pair, Spline, Keyway and Broached Profile, built like the thread catalogue, with tooth shapes generated by simulating the cutter so the model matches what the shop cuts.
- **Gear CAM:** hobbing, shaping, power skiving, 5-axis gear milling, broaching (push, stroke and rotary) and wire EDM, each with its physics.
- **Inspection from the same data:** over-pins and span measurements computed from the feature, not typed in.
- **Export control:** both files are local-only features with standards references only; nothing in them is hosted or shared.

**Novices get a thread or gear that works first time; pros get every class, process and override.** Both build on the tool, holder and machine libraries already posted here.

**Research files:** attached (MIT OR Apache-2.0), also on my fork: [cam-research-nsnlookupjn-threading.md](https://github.com/nsnlookupjn/solidcraft/blob/claude/project-thread-jfhnp0/research/cam-research-nsnlookupjn-threading.md) and [cam-research-nsnlookupjn-gear-spline-broach.md](https://github.com/nsnlookupjn/solidcraft/blob/claude/project-thread-jfhnp0/research/cam-research-nsnlookupjn-gear-spline-broach.md)
