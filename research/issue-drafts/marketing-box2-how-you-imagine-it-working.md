**What it covers**
- **A Present workspace:** a marketing kit per design: shot list, brand template, spec list and copy, built on the existing Render workspace, exploded views, motion export and configurations.
- **One button for novices:** "Make a marketing kit" gives a hero shot, turntable, explode and a datasheet from a neutral template. Pros get every camera, light, pass and batch option, all as commands.
- **A web explorer to embed on a product page:** orbit, zoom, hotspots, explode slider, colour configurator, animated controls and guided tours. The default tier sends only pre-rendered images, never geometry. A mesh tier with AR comes only after checks pass.
- **Never enough to reverse engineer:** all marketing is made from a scrubbed marketing variant. No internals, simplified features, no orthographic views or measuring, image caps. An automatic check compares every output with the master and refuses release if it is too close.
- **Off-scale variants and canary details** to spot copies made from marketing material. Every number comes from approved spec data, and those visuals are labelled "illustrative, not to scale".
- **Brochures and datasheets that stay true:** every claim links to its evidence. When the design changes, outputs go stale and must be re-approved.
- **Controlled designs** (ITAR, EAR, CUI) use the marketing-derivative release gate from the high-security research.

**Most of the engine already exists.** The missing pieces are the pipeline, video encoding, a glTF writer, the explorer and the safety check. All of it is opt-in, so no defaults change.
